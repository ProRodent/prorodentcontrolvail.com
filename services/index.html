Most rodent problems involve several tasks. An animal has to be found, removed, kept out and cleaned up after, and each of those takes a different skill. This page sorts the twelve services by the question you are likely asking.

If you only know that something is making noise, start with an inspection. If you already know the animal, go to the mouse or rat page. Cars, garages and yards have their own pages because the work and the risks differ.

How the twelve services fit together.

A typical job runs from inspection to trapping, then sealing, then cleanup and repair. Sealing comes before cleanup because there is no point sanitizing an attic that animals can still enter.

You may need only one step. A homeowner who already closed every gap may want cleanup alone, and a business may want monitoring alone. Tell the person on the phone what has been done so far and the plan skips it.

There is no separate emergency page because urgency is not a different service. The line is open 24/7, and any page here can start with a same-day request where the schedule allows.

Questions about these services

Which service should I ask about first?

If you are not sure what you have, ask for an inspection. It names the animal, finds the entry points and gives you a plan before you commit to anything else.

Can I book only the sealing or only the cleanup?

Yes. Tell the person on the phone what is already done and the visit is scoped around what remains.

Do you work on vehicles and in yards?

Yes. The vehicle page covers engine bays and cabin air systems, and the outdoor page covers burrows, sheds, plantings and feeders.

Is there a service for businesses?

The commercial page covers monitoring, treatment and written visit notes for shops, offices and food businesses.
