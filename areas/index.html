Questions about the coverage area

What if my ZIP code is not on your list?

Call and give the street address. Coverage is based on ZIP codes, so we confirm the address before booking rather than guess.

Why only four Tucson ZIP codes?

Tucson is a large city, and most of it lies farther than about fifteen miles from Vail. We list only the ZIP codes 85747, 85756, 85730 and 85734.

Is Vail a city?

No. Vail is an unincorporated community in Pima County, so county government handles local rules. Attempts to incorporate did not succeed.

Do you have pages for Vail neighborhoods?

No. We could not verify named neighborhoods in Vail, and we do not invent them. Every Vail address is handled under the one ZIP code on the list.
